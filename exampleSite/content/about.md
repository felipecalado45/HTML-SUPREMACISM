---
title: About XMin Journal
---

XMin Journal is a small, independent archive of notes on making things. It is a
place for experiments, useful links, and observations that are worth keeping
around after the workday ends.

The journal is organized into two kinds of entries:

- **Notes** are short field observations and works in progress.
- **Posts** are longer essays and detailed walkthroughs.

New entries are added when there is something useful to share, rather than on a
fixed schedule. You can browse the [latest notes](/note/) or [longer posts](/post/),
and subscribe through the [RSS feed](/index.xml).

This site is intentionally simple: clear writing, a lightweight design, and
enough room for readers to follow their own train of thought.
